# Is it pie? — where the facts come from

Every hidden fact in `isItPie.json` was checked against the source below in November 2026. Facts that only one side's people claim are worded as "X says…" so the card stays true.

## Shared definitions

- **British Pie Awards** (wholly encased in pastry; open tops not judged, lid hole up to 25mm): https://britishpieawards.co.uk/about-the-awards/pie-classes/
- **American Heritage Dictionary, pie** (over, under or surrounded by a crust; second sense a cream-filled layer cake): https://www.ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=pie
- **American Heritage Dictionary, tart** (shallow sides, no top crust; chiefly British "a pie"): https://www.ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=tart
- **Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, pie** (pastry on the bottom, sides and top): https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/pie
- **Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, tart** ("an open pie"): https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/tart_1
